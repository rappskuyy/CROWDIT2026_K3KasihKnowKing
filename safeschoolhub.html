Water & Cognitive Function
Learn how staying hydrated directly impacts your focus and memory performance.
Studies show even 1-2% dehydration impairs short-term memory, attention span, and reaction time. Aim for steady sips throughout the day rather than chugging large amounts.
Hydration for PE Classes
Optimal intake strategies for sports days and high-activity periods.
Drink ~500ml two hours before activity and 200ml every 15 minutes during exertion. Add a pinch of salt for sessions over 60 minutes.
