Water & Cognitive Function
Learn how staying hydrated impacts focus and memory.
Studies show even 1–2% dehydration impairs short-term memory, attention span, and reaction time. Aim for steady sips throughout the day rather than chugging large amounts at once.
Hydration for PE Classes
Optimal intake strategies for sports days.
Drink ~500ml two hours before activity and 200ml every 15 minutes during exertion. Add a pinch of salt for sessions over 60 minutes to replenish electrolytes.
